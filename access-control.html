Access Control

Access Control Installation

Control who can enter your building, which doors they can open, and when, without handing out keys.

Better than a ring of keys

Keys get copied, lost and never returned. An access control system replaces them with cards, key fobs, keypad codes or intercom entry, so you decide exactly who can open each door and at what times, and can remove access instantly.

Computified installs and configures access control systems and can connect them with your camera system so entries can be reviewed alongside video.

What’s included

  • Site review and door-by-door planning
  • Card, key fob and keypad reader installation
  • Electric strikes and magnetic lock installation
  • Video intercom and door-entry systems
  • Door schedules (for example, unlocked during set hours)
  • User setup: adding and removing cards and codes
  • Integration with existing camera systems where supported
  • Service and changes to existing access control systems
