Remote Access Management

Remote Access Management for Camera Systems

Secure access to your cameras from anywhere, set up and managed for you as an ongoing annual service.

Your cameras, on your phone, managed properly

Being able to check your cameras from a phone or laptop is one of the most useful parts of a security system, and one of the easiest to get wrong. Default passwords, shared logins and outdated firmware can leave a camera system open to outsiders.

Computified sets up remote viewing the right way and then manages it on an ongoing basis: adding and removing users, keeping access secure, and helping when something stops connecting.

What’s included

  • Remote viewing setup on phones, tablets and computers
  • Individual user accounts with appropriate permissions
  • Adding and removing users as staff or family changes
  • Password and access review
  • Firmware and app update checks for supported equipment
  • Troubleshooting when remote viewing stops working
  • Support for systems we install and many existing systems
