Security Cameras & CCTV

Security Camera System Installation

Professionally installed camera systems, with cloud-based video storage offered as an ongoing annual plan.

See what’s happening, and keep the footage

Computified designs and installs CCTV / IP camera systems sized to your space, from a few cameras at an entrance to full coverage of a building or campus. We help choose camera placement, cabling and recording equipment that fit the site.

Recordings matter most when something goes wrong. With cloud-based video storage, footage is stored off-site, so it’s still available even if a recorder on-site is damaged or stolen.

What’s included

  • Site walk-through and camera placement planning
  • Indoor and outdoor camera installation
  • Cabling, mounting and network connection
  • Recorder (NVR) setup and configuration
  • Cloud-based video storage setup
  • Live view and playback setup on phone and computer
  • Walk-through of how to use the system
  • Repairs and additions to existing camera systems

Cloud Video Storage Plan

Cloud video storage is an ongoing, recurring service, offered on an annual plan. It keeps recording to the cloud long after the installation is finished.

  • Off-site storage of camera recordings
  • Access to stored footage from authorized devices
  • Plan sized to your number of cameras and how long you need footage kept
  • Renews annually, so storage keeps running year after year

Pricing depends on the number of cameras and the retention period. Contact us for a quote.
