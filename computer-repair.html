Computer Repair & IT

Computer Repair & IT Services

Diagnosis, repair and ongoing IT support for computers, networks and the devices that depend on them.

Keep your technology working

When a computer slows down, a printer stops talking to the network, or the Wi‑Fi drops in half the building, work stops. Computified diagnoses the problem, fixes it, and helps prevent it from coming back.

We support individual machines as well as whole offices, and we provide ongoing network maintenance so the systems you rely on, including security cameras and door controllers, stay connected.

What’s included

  • Desktop and laptop diagnosis and repair
  • Hardware upgrades: memory, storage (SSD) and component replacement
  • Virus and malware removal
  • Operating system installation, updates and cleanup
  • Data backup setup and file transfer to new computers
  • New computer, printer and peripheral setup
  • Network and Wi‑Fi setup and troubleshooting
  • Ongoing network maintenance for offices and facilities (see Network Management)
  • Email and account setup
