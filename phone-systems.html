Phone Systems

Business Phone Systems

Modern VoIP / cloud phone systems, installed, set up and supported, so every call reaches the right person.

A phone system that fits how you work

Computified sets up VoIP and cloud-based phone systems that run over your internet connection instead of traditional phone lines. Calls can ring at desk phones, on a computer or on a mobile app, and the system can be adjusted as your needs change.

We handle the details: choosing and installing handsets, building your call routing and auto attendant, setting up extensions and voicemail, and moving your existing phone numbers over, then we stay available for ongoing support.

What’s included

  • VoIP / cloud phone system planning and setup
  • Installation and configuration of desk phones and handsets
  • Extensions for staff, departments and rooms
  • Call routing: ring groups, business hours and after-hours rules
  • Auto attendant (“Press 1 for…” menus) setup
  • Voicemail setup, including voicemail-to-email where supported
  • Moving (porting) your existing phone numbers
  • Network checks so calls sound clear
  • Ongoing support: adding users, changing call flows and troubleshooting
